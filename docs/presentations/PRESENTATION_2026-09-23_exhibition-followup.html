Resident Agents as Evaluators

Follow-up · 23 September

Jungwon Park

One week on from 16 September. Three days in Eunjeom Village now run end to end.

What works now
02

Three days now run
end to end.

Day 1 has three requests, day 2 has six, day 3 has eight. At the end of each day you pick one of three changes, so there are 27 paths.

People move on the real road map now. I pulled 268 points out of the source map, and every route is the shortest path on it.

All 27 paths are written out in advance — 39 nodes, 2,285 lines of talk, 202 resident comments. Nothing calls a model while a visitor is standing there.

The end of the day
03

At ten at night the clock stops, and the residents say what happened to them.

Rules and model
04

The rules decide what happens.
The model only writes the words.

Rules say where each person is, whether they can go, whether they say yes or no, how long the walk takes, and which three changes come up at the end of the day.

The model writes the talk, and the one line each resident leaves at ten at night. That is all it does. I never ask it who should help, or whether the day went well.

A script re-reads all 39 nodes and looks for times nobody recorded, people talking who are not there, gaps in where someone is, comments in the wrong mouth, and choices that lead to the same day anyway. It found none of them.

What is not done
05

Three things are still open.

1. Resident behaviour

Not started

Replies and day ratings still come from rules. Persona agents deciding for themselves has not moved at all, and that is the part the research claim needs.

2. Ten days

Works for three

Ten days needs the rule values grouped into steps, so that branches merge and I can still write them out in advance.

3. The venue

Not started

The tablet and the projector are still one window on my laptop. They have to come apart before the rehearsal on 11 October.

The other two from September — offering three changes, and carrying them into the next day — both work now.

In closing
06

Three days run on the real map,
and the change you pick shows up the next morning.
