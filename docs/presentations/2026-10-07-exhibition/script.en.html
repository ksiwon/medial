Jungwon Park · Graduation Show Script

Read it as it is · about 4 minutes · slides 1–7 · short sentences on purpose
1
Cover20 sec

Hello. I am Jungwon Park, from Industrial Design. My project is a tool that lets us try a service in a pretend village before we bring it to a real village. The village behind me is that pretend village. ▶ Screen: the intro film is playing. Point to the first scene, the village at sunset.

2
What this show does40 sec

We talked with all twelve people in a small fishing village. The hospital is far. In the daytime, people are in the fields or at sea. We cannot visit again and again, so I built the village inside a computer. Today, you are a service designer. You check an AI service for three days. You watch, you read what the villagers say, and you choose one request.

3
One visit30 sec

One visit takes about five minutes. All three days take about fifteen, so we show only the scenes that tell the story. The other scenes stay in the list below. At the end of each day, the villagers speak. Then you choose a request, and your choice becomes the next day's rule. ▶ Screen: the first day starts by itself after the intro film.

4
Demo · the key scene60 sec

This is day three, in the morning. Two brothers are at the pier. One says, “My chest feels a little tight.” Then he says it is nothing. Look at the right side of the screen. It says, “The AI cannot see this scene.” Nobody told the AI. That evening, there is an emergency at the same man's house. His brother says, “I did not ask more this morning.” People who stay quiet are not seen by the system. This is the scene I most want you to see. ▶ Screen: on day three, press “next scene →” (inside the ⚙ settings). It jumps to the pier at 10:56, then to the emergency at 20:30. Point to the words “the AI cannot see this scene.”

5
Demo · you choose60 sec

At the end of a day, the villagers speak. Each person talks only about what happened to them. There is no score and no ranking. A person who had no part in it is marked “not involved.” Then you choose a request. It is not a number. It is a sentence, like “Protect the person being checked.” Each card shows why the request came up, and which villager lived through it. ▶ Screen: end of day one → press “read the reviews” → “what to change” → the visitor presses one of the three cards.

6
In the hall25 sec

Today it is one screen, and you choose with your own hand. The show does not call an AI while you watch. The next day was already made. So whatever you choose, the next day is really there. After the three days, if nobody touches the screen for sixty seconds, it goes back to the film.

7
What we say25 sec

One more thing. Everything on the screen is pretend. The people and the talk are made up, and the names are not real. These opinions are not real villagers' opinions. And real villagers have not checked this yet. At the next visit, I will ask them first. Thank you.

If someone asks a question

  • “Is this what real people said?” — No. They are made-up villagers, built from interviews. Real checks so far: zero.
  • “Does it replace people?” — No. It helps between visits to the village.
  • “Does your choice change the result?” — I do not say that. In 8 of 9 paths the day-three numbers are the same. Only one path was different: ask neighbors first, 40 minutes, six refusals. The reason is that only one person can move in the daytime.
  • “Is three days a long study?” — No. It is a simulation, not a study of real people over time.
  • “What does the AI decide?” — Only the words. Who is where, and who can go, comes from daily schedules and distance.
